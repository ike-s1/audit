Audit coverage

Checks that go beyond “the formula runs.”

Excel can calculate a wrong model perfectly. Second Review looks for structural breaks, conflicting inputs, failed reconciliations, and evidence that does not support the output.

ƒx

Formula integrity

Where the model stops behaving like its surrounding structure.

  • Broken row and period patterns
  • Pasted hardcodes in formula regions
  • Numeric constants inside formulas
  • Broken references and masked errors
Σ

Reconciliation

Whether totals and linked statements agree with their components.

  • Totals and subtotals that fail
  • Cash and balance inconsistencies
  • Cross-sheet mismatches
  • Source document discrepancies
⇄

Model consistency

Values that conflict across tabs, periods, and conventions.

  • Conflicting assumptions
  • Mixed units, signs, and currencies
  • Date convention changes
  • Duplicate or missing records
◎

Decision impact

How a suspicious cell connects to outputs that matter.

  • Downstream dependent cells
  • Material output sensitivity
  • Unusually influential changes
  • Evidence needed to confirm

Questions

The questions a careful reviewer asks.

What happens to my file?

Your workbook is never used to train models and is not changed. Before a production engagement, the exact processing location, AI provider, transmitted context, access controls, retention period, and deletion process should be confirmed in the engagement terms.

Is this an audit?

No. This is a second review that helps a qualified professional catch material errors. It does not provide formal assurance or a reliance letter.

How do you handle false positives?

Deterministic findings provide factual evidence. Grounded AI review can remove an issue only when supplied workbook context supports a false positive or intentional design. If context is insufficient, the finding remains open.

Why not paste it into ChatGPT or Copilot?

A general chat response is commentary, not a systematic verification pass. Second Review runs defined checks and gives every user-facing claim an exact workbook location.

What files are supported?

One .xlsx workbook today. VBA, external links, unsupported formulas, and unavailable capabilities are reported as limitations rather than silently skipped.

Does the tool fix the model?

No. It explains the issue and investigation step, but never writes into the workbook. You stay responsible for accepting, dismissing, or resolving each finding.
